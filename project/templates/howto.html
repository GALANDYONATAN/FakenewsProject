How the System Works?

On the Home button, click Visit our Website, and the site will open with two options:
1.Transcribe a TikTok video
2.Transcribe text in order to check its credibility.
To transcribe text, simply click Download to access the TikTok parameters used for credibility analysis. Refer only to the attribute in the text row that you need to enter in order to check its credibility. You can download the dataset from here. Enter the text you wish to select from the training set. For video transcription, you will need to provide a URL — please make sure it is a valid address. Example: https://vt.tiktok.com/ZSrG3AdKh/
